# Colosseum Crypto World's Fair 2026 / Superteam India local track

- **Slug:** colosseum-cwf-2026-superteam-india
- **Organizer / sponsor:** Colosseum (global hackathon), Superteam India (local prize pool)
- **Main hackathon page:** Colosseum Crypto World's Fair (colosseum.com / arena.colosseum.org), fetch for global rules and tracks
- **Track listing page:** Superteam Earn, Superteam India Crypto World's Fair side track
- **Deadline:** 2026-10-12 (Colosseum portal submission; confirm timezone on portal)
- **Chain / stack required:** Solana only for this pool (global hackathon is multichain)
- **Region / audience:** India-based teams

## Eligibility (hard gates)

- Built on Solana
- Team based in India
- Registered on Colosseum with "India" selected as country
- Submitted to BOTH the Colosseum portal and Superteam Earn
- Eligible under the official global rules

## Judging criteria (verbatim)

- **Ecosystem Impact:** real value to Solana through infrastructure, tooling, or a product people need
- **Product-Market Fit:** a clear problem, a defined user, and early signs of traction
- **Growth Potential:** brings new users to Solana or deepens engagement among existing ones

## Prizes and what winners really get

- 1st $2,500, 2nd $1,500, 3rd $1,000, paid on top of anything won globally
- All winners: Member role in Superteam India (network, future grants/bounties access)
- Real upside is the global result: Indian teams have won at Colosseum repeatedly and 6 made the
  Colosseum Accelerator ($200k investment). Build for the global judges; the India pool is a floor.

## Stackable tracks / bounties

- Other Crypto World's Fair side tracks on Superteam Earn (sponsor + chapter tracks)
- CLOCK IN, Solana Mobile hackathon (Seeker / dApp Store), if the idea is mobile-first
- Solana Foundation hackathon hub (e.g. Stocklana, tokenized stocks)

## What the organizer is really buying

Colosseum runs an accelerator. Global judges pick fundable startups with a real user and early
traction, not clever demos. Superteam India wants Indian teams whose product grows Solana usage,
ideally bringing new users (Indian users count) onchain. Score "company potential" and
"distribution" high. Traction before Oct 12 matters because PMF is literally a criterion.

## Local / context lenses to run

Verify each with a fetched source; treat these as questions, not facts.
- India crypto tax and rules (30% tax on VDA gains, 1% TDS, FIU-IND registration for exchanges/VASPs): what can legally be built and for whom
- Cross-border income: freelancers, agencies, creators, SaaS founders in India paid from abroad (fees, FX spread, delays)
- Remittances into India (India is the world's largest remittance recipient [verify latest World Bank number])
- UPI as the default payment habit: where stablecoins do and do not beat it (cross-border, not domestic)
- Indian exporters / MSMEs and USD invoicing
- Gaming, esports, fantasy (post-2025 online real-money gaming ban; verify what is allowed)
- Students and developers: largest dev population growth markets, Superteam India community itself
- Past Indian winners at Colosseum and what they built (use colosseum-copilot)
